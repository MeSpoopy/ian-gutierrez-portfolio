RG26 / Application workflow

Call quality evaluation

Call context, assessment criteria, reviewer comments, and a printable evaluation record.

The need

Quality reviewers need a repeatable assessment structure so review criteria and coaching observations can be compared.

The approach

An evaluation form brings together call context, weighted categories, reviewer responses, comments, and a printable report.

Visible workflow

  • Record the evaluator, employee, company, call date, and duration.
  • Assess opening criteria with remarks, Yes/No responses, and comments.
  • Display category and total scores and prepare a printable evaluation report.

Intended value

A shared structure can support consistent reviews and more specific coaching discussions.
