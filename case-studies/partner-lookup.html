A Zoho Creator application retrieves partner-company details by phone number, replacing manual searches through a source spreadsheet.

The problem

Administrators manually searched Zoho Sheet to locate partner-company information. They needed a direct lookup that returned the relevant details in a consistent format.

The approach

I imported the company data into Zoho Creator and built a lookup around phone-number selection. Selecting a number displays the matching entity, company name, and phone details in an HTML/CSS summary, while the underlying company fields remain read-only.

Implementation highlights

  • Deluge workflows initialize the form and hide internal fields and default controls.
  • Read-only company fields prevent edits through the lookup interface.
  • A dedicated results view separates the search experience from record storage.

The outcome

Administrators can retrieve a matching company record directly instead of opening and scanning the source spreadsheet.
