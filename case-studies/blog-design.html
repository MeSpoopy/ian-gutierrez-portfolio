BritePH blog design

The problem

Readers need to discover useful business content and move easily from article summaries into the full story.

The approach

A branded landing view features a prominent article. Repeatable cards organize the wider collection, while a consistent article layout supports longer content.

Workflow details

  • Feature Zoho CRM content on the landing view.
  • Browse image cards with headlines and summaries.
  • Read an article with a lead image, author information, and sharing controls.

Intended value

The design provides a reusable publishing pattern and a consistent way to browse the content collection.
