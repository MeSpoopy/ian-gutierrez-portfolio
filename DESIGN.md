## Overview

The connection studio identity uses three interlocking channels, editorial typography and actual project evidence. The approved September 2026 refinement replaces green and lime with navy and soft blue while retaining the connection motif, font pairing, portraits and project materials.

Surface mode: Experience. Visitors see the work, understand Ian’s contribution and find contact quickly. Selected work precedes the compact approach. Illustrations explain relationships; screenshots document real interfaces and configurations.

## Colors

Soft blue marks action, selection, focus and limited heading emphasis. Most headings are near-white; body and secondary text use readable cool neutrals. Navy elevation replaces green panels.

Use dark labels on blue buttons: #0E141B on #6E9FC7 has 6.56:1 contrast. White on the same blue is unsuitable for normal text. Decorative divider #293746 is subtle; controls needing a visible boundary use #647687. Amber is reserved for small recognition details.

Preserve original screenshot, credential and third-party logo colors.

## Typography

Self-hosted Manrope supplies headings; DM Sans supplies body and labels. Reading copy is 16px on mobile, typically 16–18px on desktop, with line-height around 1.6–1.7. Meaningful captions and metadata start at 13px. Mobile hero type is around 40–48px; desktop display stays below 6rem. Tracking stays at or above -0.04em. Allow natural wrapping.

## Layout

Use shared editorial alignment and an 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96px spacing rhythm. Desktop section padding is generally 80–96px; mobile 48–64px. Mobile gutters are 20px. Correct component bounds rather than hiding document overflow.

Homepage order: Hero and early Vendor Map preview → Selected work and all-project disclosure → Compact approach → About and experience → Compact education → Selected credentials → Contact.

The three main projects keep distinct compositions while concise facts explain problem, role, delivery and supported outcome. Case-study evidence appears before detailed implementation.

Preserve all fourteen case-study URLs, skip links and native scrolling. Narrow layouts stack content and keep diagrams understandable without motion.

## Elevation & Depth

Navy surfaces distinguish chapters and previews. Editorial rules organize content. Depth belongs to the reflective sculpture and layered connection geometry. The image viewer uses a dark backdrop and protected focus.

## Shapes

Three rounded rectangular channels remain the signature. Blue, slate and off-white materials recur in the hero, approach and closing mark. Evidence/viewer surfaces have restrained rounded corners; primary actions use consistent pills.

## Components

Sticky navigation has a named mobile menu with expanded state and Escape handling. Work leads browsing; contact remains accessible.

Capability buttons expose a single selected state and update plain-text explanations and real project links. Artwork controls are secondary. Small screens initially show still artwork; a visitor may explicitly request 3D. Reduced-motion preferences have a complete static presentation.

The 3D scene uses demand rendering and stops after pointer/selection responses settle. Paused, offscreen and hidden-page states suspend motion. Static artwork covers loading until a real frame is ready and survives graphics failure.

The approach has three concise steps and a completed static diagram on small screens. Optional native-scroll motion never gates access to content.

Screenshots and credentials open named native dialogs with zoom, original-image links, Escape and focus return. Preserve distinctions between documented implementation, intended benefit and proposed application.

The latest role has a visible summary. Older experience, toolkit and older learning use disclosures. Education retains the actual portrait/logo and factual background in a compact treatment.

Contact includes a visibly labeled Email Ian action, address, announced copy feedback or recovery, LinkedIn and telephone. Public footer navigation has legal pages and a top shortcut. Admin remains directly accessible at its existing URL outside visitor navigation.

Aim for comfortable 44px targets, visible focus, readable names and restrained hover/pressed feedback.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Keep soft blue/navy, the fonts and the connected-channel motif.
- Bring actual evidence forward and explain ownership.
- Preserve all fourteen URLs and supplied portraits, logos and credentials.
- Retain original evidence colors and caveats.
- Never invent impact, dates, endorsements or production status.
- Never intercept scrolling or require animation to read.
- Never use white labels on the soft-blue primary button.
