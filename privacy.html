Who operates this site

Ian Van Anthony Gutierrez, based in Cagayan de Oro, Philippines, operates this professional portfolio and is responsible for the personal information received through portfolio enquiries. You can contact me at ianvan.gutierrez@gmail.com.

Website visits

This portfolio is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives technical information to deliver pages and images, and logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security, including when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. GitHub describes its handling of this information in the GitHub Privacy Statement ↗.

The website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Google receives the technical requests needed to deliver those fonts, including your IP address and browser information. See Google Fonts privacy information ↗.

The portfolio itself does not set advertising cookies, request precise device location, or record your screen, keystrokes, or contact details while you browse. Email, telephone, and social links open the service you choose; there is no contact form that submits information in the background.

Visitor analytics

Visitor analytics are not currently active. No visitor counter or location-tracking script has been connected to this portfolio. This section will be updated before analytics collection begins.

GitHub’s hosting and security processing described above still applies when you visit.

Contact enquiries

If you email, call, or message me, I receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, contact details, message, and project requirements. I use it to respond, discuss a potential engagement, and maintain relevant correspondence.

Where applicable, this processing supports steps you request before entering a contract, an existing engagement, or the legitimate interest of responding to professional enquiries. Please share only information needed for your enquiry and avoid sending sensitive records through an initial message.

Providers & retention

Hosting, font delivery, email, and social platforms process information under their own terms and privacy policies. They may process data outside your country, including outside the Philippines. Following an external link brings you under that service’s privacy practices.

I do not sell enquiry information or use it for advertising profiles. Correspondence is used for the enquiry or engagement concerned and may be disclosed where legally required. It is retained only while needed for that purpose, related records, or applicable legal obligations. You may ask me to delete information that is no longer needed.

Retention of technical service logs is controlled by the relevant provider. This portfolio does not make those logs publicly available.

Your choices & rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information I hold about you; object to processing; or withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. Some information may need to be retained for legal obligations or an ongoing engagement.

Email ianvan.gutierrez@gmail.com with your request. I may need enough information to verify that it concerns your own data. You may also raise a concern with the Philippine National Privacy Commission ↗ or your local data protection authority.

Policy updates

This policy will be revised when the website’s data practices change. Check the date at the top for the latest update. The Terms & Conditions explain use of the portfolio and its content.
